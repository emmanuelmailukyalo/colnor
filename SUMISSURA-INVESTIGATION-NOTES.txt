SUMISSURA WEBSITE INVESTIGATION NOTES
Observed: 12 October 2026 (Africa/Nairobi)
Scope: Public website behavior and visual observations. No site construction in this investigation.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
This report records observations from the accessible public site. It does not claim to have inspected every page, every product, every image, every responsive breakpoint, or every hover state. The site contains thousands of dynamic products and the full designer has many configurable options. Exact image-by-image inventory and every button state require a longer controlled pass. Values marked “observed” came from the browser or computed styles; visual estimates are labeled accordingly. No account, personal data, purchase, or payment interaction was submitted.

12. DOM / COMPONENT SHAPE NOTES (OBSERVED + INFERRED)
These are component-level notes based on the live DOM/accessibility tree and visual layout. The production page is dynamically generated and its exact class/component tree changes between pages, so this is not a full source-code/CSS extraction.

Shared page shell
- Announcement region: a compact full-width strip at the top with centered text; dark charcoal fill on the home/collection landing state. Product-detail view uses a white header with no visible announcement strip in the inspected viewport.
- Header/navigation: semantic links/buttons and image-based SVG icons. Home/collection landing header is layered above the hero. Product detail/configurator use a compact utility header. Logo image is an SVG asset in the live page.
- Main page content is composed of full-width section bands with centered max-width content; background alternates between white and warm off-white. The hero/media sections are edge-to-edge. Card grids/carousels sit inside page gutters.
- Collection page uses at least: hero container; “main_products” style category carousel; three-column how-it-works content; split promotional/editorial blocks; inspiration carousel; a product-capsule grid; trust/review blocks; editorial cards; long-form copy/accordion; footer.
- Product listing card is a link wrapping product image, product name, fabric/color/pattern attributes, badges, and price. Listing cards are square-cornered at the observed size. The cards are consistently portrait oriented.
- Product detail is a two-column product media/info layout. Gallery is the left primary column; breadcrumb and product purchase facts are in the right column. Share/wishlist are icon controls. Product configurator is a full-screen split workspace with a left options/filter pane and right rendered garment preview.
- Bottom sticky collection CTA is a fixed full-width white component with logo, product/starting price and CTA. Chat is a separate floating circular pink action near the lower-right viewport edge.
