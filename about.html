ABOUT COLNOR

Tailoring for the way women live now.

Colnor began with a simple idea: a great suit should feel considered, versatile, and easy to make your own.

We are building a focused women’s suit collection that brings together clean silhouettes, wearable colors, and straightforward shopping. From weekday tailoring to occasion dressing, each piece is selected to help women feel comfortable and confident in their own style.

Colnor is an independent storefront project in development. Our collection, local delivery details, and customer services will continue to grow as the shop prepares for launch.
